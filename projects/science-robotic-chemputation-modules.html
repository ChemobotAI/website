Robotic Chemputation Modules

Practical building blocks for digital chemistry

Before a lab can benefit from AI, it needs reliable digital interfaces to experiments. This work focused on robotic modules that standardize common chemical operations and make them programmable across molecules, materials, nanomaterials, and formulations.

For a consulting audience, this page is framed less as a single publication and more as an engineering philosophy: robust automation comes from modularity, repeatable interfaces, and software-controlled workflows that scientists can actually use.

The project provides a useful bridge between research prototypes and lab infrastructure: a practical language for designing automated chemistry systems that can scale beyond a single bespoke machine.

// Published in ACS Central Science, 2023. Daniel Salley first author.
